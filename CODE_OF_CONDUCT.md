# Code of Conduct

OpenReshoot should be a practical and respectful project space.

## Standards

Expected behavior:

- Be direct, respectful, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, product behavior, documentation, and technical tradeoffs.
- Assume contributors are working in good faith unless there is clear evidence
  otherwise.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private information without permission.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized comments or imagery in project spaces.
- Repeated disruption of technical discussion.
- Sharing private credentials, personal data, or unreleased user content.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may lead to
temporary or permanent blocking from the project.

To report a problem, open a private security advisory or contact a maintainer
through the GitHub repository owner account.
